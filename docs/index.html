States and Union Territories

NITI Aayog scores every State and UT from 0 to 100 by combining many indicators. Higher means closer to the 2030 targets.

This is a composite index, not a measured value. NITI Aayog normalises and weights indicators to build each score, so the index is kept separate from the indicator data. Scores were read from the report's pages 67–68 and checked against figures stated in its text.
  1. Find your StateHover over the map, or find it in the ranking beside it.
  2. Read its score0 to 100; 65 or more is a "Front Runner" in NITI Aayog's terms.

About the district data

  • Indicators come from the National Family Health Survey district fact sheets (Ministry of Health and Family Welfare / IIPS). District estimates come from a sample survey and have sampling error, so small differences between districts or rounds may not be meaningful.
  • Values in brackets in the fact sheets are based on 25–49 cases; "na" and "*" mean no estimate was published. Districts formed after 2015-16 have no NFHS-4 value.
  • Poverty level is NITI Aayog's district headcount ratio from the National Multidimensional Poverty Index 2023 (NFHS-5 round). Districts are split into five equal-sized groups by that figure; the ranges are shown in the filter.
  • The multidimensional poverty index is itself built from NFHS data, including nutrition, child mortality, schooling, cooking fuel, sanitation, drinking water, electricity, housing, assets, bank accounts and maternal health. For indicators that measure one of these, a strong link with poverty is partly built in; those indicators are marked.
  • Aspirational Districts are the 112 districts on NITI Aayog's list; 4 districts created after NFHS-5 cannot be linked to fact sheet data.
  • Group summaries are medians across districts, not population totals. Districts differ greatly in population, so they are not State or national figures.
  • Population, area and urban share (Census 2011) are not included yet: the Census website could not be reached to download the official tables.

SDG Explorer India: a verified open pipeline and interactive atlas of official Sustainable Development Goal data for India, from national series to 704 districts. It describes every source, how each was read and checked, how much of India's SDG data is missing, and what the district data show about poverty. Data rules

  • Nothing is invented, imputed or interpolated. Missing years stay missing.
  • Values are kept as published. Values published as text (such as <0.1) stay text.
  • Composite index scores (NITI Aayog) never mix with measured indicator data.
  • Observation years are kept separate from publication dates.
  • UN and MoSPI series for the same indicator stay separate.
