SDG Explorer India

India's progress on the UN Sustainable Development Goals, built only from official sources: the UN Global SDG Indicators Database, MoSPI's National Indicator Framework report, NITI Aayog's SDG India Index and the National Family Health Survey district fact sheets. Every value is shown as published. Missing values stay missing; nothing is filled in.

National indicators

Pick a goal, then a data series. One SDG indicator can have several series (for example by measure or age group). Breakdowns such as sex, age or urban/rural are kept exactly as published; choose up to 8 to compare.

Values as published

NITI Aayog SDG India Index 2023-24 Composite index

NITI Aayog scores each State and Union Territory from 0 to 100 by combining many indicators, normalised and weighted. It is a composite score, not a measured value, so it is kept apart from the indicator data. Scores were read from the report's page images (pages 67–68) and checked against figures stated in the report.

Map boundaries: DataMeet India community (CC BY 4.0), simplified; illustrative only, not an authoritative depiction of India's borders.

India's index scores by edition Composite index

India's composite score and its score on each goal in the four editions of the index. Index editions are publication rounds, not observation years, and NITI Aayog changed the indicator set between editions, so differences are not pure trends.

Districts NFHS-5 and NFHS-4

National Family Health Survey district fact sheets (Ministry of Health and Family Welfare / IIPS) for 34 indicators with a clear SDG counterpart: NFHS-5 (2019-21) and NFHS-4 (2015-16). District estimates come from a sample survey and have sampling error. Values in brackets in the fact sheets are based on 25–49 cases; "na" and "*" mean no estimate was published. Districts formed after 2015-16 have no NFHS-4 value. No State totals are calculated from district values.

Validation

Data rules

  • Nothing is invented, imputed or interpolated. Missing years stay missing.
  • Values are kept as published. Values published as text (such as <0.1) stay text.
  • Composite index scores (NITI Aayog) never mix with raw indicator data.
  • Observation years are kept separate from publication dates.
  • UN and MoSPI series for the same indicator stay separate.
