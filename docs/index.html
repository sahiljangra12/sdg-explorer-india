States and Union Territories

NITI Aayog scores every State and UT from 0 to 100 by combining many indicators. Higher means closer to the 2030 targets.

This is a composite index, not a measured value. NITI Aayog normalises and weights indicators to build each score, so the index is kept separate from the indicator data. Scores were read from the report's pages 67–68 and checked against figures stated in its text.

About the district data

National Family Health Survey district fact sheets (Ministry of Health and Family Welfare / IIPS), for 34 indicators with a clear SDG counterpart. District estimates come from a sample survey and have sampling error. Values in brackets are based on 25–49 cases; "na" and "*" mean no estimate was published. Districts formed after 2015-16 have no NFHS-4 value. No State totals are calculated from district values.

Data rules

  • Nothing is invented, imputed or interpolated. Missing years stay missing.
  • Values are kept as published. Values published as text (such as <0.1) stay text.
  • Composite index scores (NITI Aayog) never mix with measured indicator data.
  • Observation years are kept separate from publication dates.
  • UN and MoSPI series for the same indicator stay separate.
